# App Specification

## What
A quiz app about Thai street food for high school students.

## Who
Thai high school students aged 13-18, using a mobile browse.

## Why
Students forget food culture knowledge before exams.
Imediate feedback helps them identify mistakes right away.

## Features 
1. 5 multiple-choice questions about Thai street food
2. 4 choices per question
3. Show correct answer immediately after each submission
4. Score counter at the top updating after each answer
5. Retry button at the end to restart the quiz

## Look
Colourful card design. Large readable text. Mobile-friendly layout.
Use Thai language throughtout.

## How 
No login required. No database. Runs in browser only. Python flask.
